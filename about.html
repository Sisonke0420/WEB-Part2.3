Since 2008 · Free State, South Africa

A shelter is a beginning,
not an ending.

Free State Animal Rescue began with one borrowed kennel and a stubborn belief that no animal should be invisible.

Today, our team supports vulnerable animals through rescue, rehabilitation, education, and thoughtful adoptions. We meet animals where they are and work at the pace their trust requires.
